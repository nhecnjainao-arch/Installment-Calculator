🧮 Calculation Formula

Profit:
Borrowed Amount × Profit Rate

Processing Fee:
Borrowed Amount × Processing Fee Rate

Total Payable:
Borrowed Amount + Profit + Processing Fee

Monthly Installment:
Total Payable ÷ Number of Months

Late Payment Charge:
Amount Due × Daily Late Rate × Number of Delayed Days
